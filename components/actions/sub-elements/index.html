07 — Elevation

The Borderless Sub Element

A sub element with no border at all. It is still a sub element in every other respect — flat at rest, and on hover it lifts and its shadow appears. It is the tier to reach for when something should be present without competing for attention.

With a border

The default sub element: a real 4px inline border, flat at rest, lifting on hover.
