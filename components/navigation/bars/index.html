Bars & Frames

The two places navigation can live: pinned to the top of the page, or docked at the bottom as a floating bar of sub buttons. Both are real components — copy them out of the page.

Top bar — fp-navbar

A sticky surface bar. The links are borderless sub elements; the active one takes a real border without moving anything.

Floating bottom nav — fp-island-nav

A detached pill that floats over the page. The container is a main element — no border, a hard shadow, pressing down on hover; the items inside it are ordinary fp-btn--sub buttons, re-sized to the row. The current destination wears the accent border, the one sanctioned emphasis.

Mobile Bars
