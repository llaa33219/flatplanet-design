01 — Foundations

The Rules

Five non-negotiable rules define every FlatPlanet component. Break one and the system stops feeling like itself.

36px Radius Ceiling

36 px is the maximum radius anywhere in the system. Smaller radii are permitted only when a short height forces it — a 36 px-tall control cannot carry a 36 px radius and still read as a control.

Zero Gradients

Gradients are banned outright. Depth comes from flat fills plus solid offset shadows — the geometry does the work, never the colour transition.

Hard Shadows

Every shadow is a solid offset shadow with zero blur, offset equally and positively on both axes — it always falls to the bottom-right. Main elements carry the shadow and no border. Sub elements carry a real inline border and no resting shadow at all. On hover, the main shadow collapses to 2 px and the element presses down; sub elements lift and their shadow appears.

Three Speeds

0.1 s fast, 0.2 s default, 0.3 s slow. That is the entire timing vocabulary. Anything else is a bug.

BLP Colours Only

Every colour comes from the BLP palette. #fefeff for most surfaces, #000309 for dark, #007bff for accent.

Sub Elements Lift

When a parent hovers, its sub elements rise by the sub-shadow height and gain a hard shadow of their own — the depth hierarchy stays readable at every level. A rising element never loses its shadow.

The shadow contract

Every shadow in the library is (positive px) (positive px) 0 <ink> — both offsets positive and equal, zero blur, zero spread. A shadow always falls to the bottom-right.

There is no upward shadow, no leftward shadow, and no four-sided band: a positive spread paints ink on all four sides, which is a border, not a shadow. A variant is a different size, never a different direction.

Accent on a sub element's border

The accent never makes a sub element bigger or moves it. It recolours the one border that is already there, so the eye goes to that element and nowhere else.

One mechanism, one modifier: fp-sub--accent. It works on any sub element.

Accent on text

When the accent emphasises words, the words themselves turn bold and take the accent colour — emphasis is worn by the type, never painted behind it.

The emphasised words are bold and blue, and the rest of the sentence is not.
